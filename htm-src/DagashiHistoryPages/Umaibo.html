The Story of Umaibo

Umaibo (うまい棒), literally meaning "delicious stick," is one of Japan's most iconic and beloved dagashi snacks. This cylindrical corn puff snack has captured hearts with its unique texture, variety of flavors, and incredibly affordable price point.

Fun Fact: Umaibo has maintained its ¥10 (around SGD 0.10) price point for most of its history, making it a symbol of affordable happiness in Japanese snack culture!

The Umaibo Journey

1979: Birth of an Icon

Umaibo was first created by Yoshikazu Ebisawa of Yaokin Corporation. The initial concept was to create a snack that was both delicious and affordable for children. The first flavor was Corn Potage, which remains popular to this day.

The name "Umaibo" was chosen for its straightforward meaning: "うまい" (delicious) + "棒" (stick).

1980s: Flavor Revolution

Throughout the 1980s, Umaibo expanded its flavor lineup dramatically. New varieties like Mentai (spicy cod roe), Cheese, and Tonkatsu (pork cutlet) were introduced, reflecting popular Japanese tastes.

By the end of the 1980s, Umaibo had become a cultural phenomenon, with children collecting different flavors and trading them with friends.

1990s-2000s: Cultural Icon

Umaibo became more than just a snack - it became a cultural icon. Limited edition flavors, collaborations with anime series, and special regional varieties helped maintain its popularity even as Japan's snack market became more sophisticated.

Umaibo's mascot character, "Umaemon," became widely recognized and loved, appearing on merchandise and in advertisements.

Present Day: Global Recognition

Today, Umaibo continues to delight snack lovers worldwide. With over 30 regular flavors and numerous limited editions, it represents the fun and innovative spirit of Japanese dagashi culture. The snack has gained international recognition, becoming a must-try item for visitors to Japan.

While maintaining its traditional charm, Umaibo continues to innovate with new flavors and collaborations, bridging generations of snack lovers.

Umaibo Fun Facts

Production Process

Each Umaibo is made through a special extrusion process that creates its unique cylindrical shape and airy texture!

Regional Specialties

Different regions in Japan have their own exclusive Umaibo flavors, reflecting local specialties and tastes!

Global Appeal

Umaibo has fans worldwide, with some flavors becoming collector's items among international snack enthusiasts!
