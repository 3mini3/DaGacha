Dagashi Story

Savory

Umaibo

Japan's most iconic dagashi! This crunchy corn snack comes in countless flavors and has been a beloved treat for decades. A perfect introduction to Japanese snack culture!

Fun Fact

Launched in 1979, Umaibo has released over 100 different flavors! The corn potage flavor remains the most popular choice among fans worldwide.

Chocolate Dagashi Collection

Chocolate-flavored dagashi offer rich taste experiences in creative forms. Tirol Choco delivers premium chocolate taste in bite-sized pieces, while uniquely-shaped treats add fun to snacking!

Tirol Choco launched in 1962, named after Austria's Tyrol region! These creative chocolate treats showcase Japan's innovative approach to confectionery design.
